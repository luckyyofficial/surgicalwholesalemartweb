A little clarity goes a long way.

Practical checklists for an organised procurement conversation.

PROCUREMENT GUIDE · 01

Start with the specification

Write down your requirements before comparing products.

Read the checklist

List the intended department, available space, dimensions, quantities, power requirements and existing equipment connections. Ask the supplier to confirm the exact configuration and included accessories before placing an order.

Discuss your requirements →
PROCUREMENT GUIDE · 02

Read a quotation with confidence

Compare the full scope of supply, not just the headline amount.

Read the checklist

Request an itemised quotation covering the product configuration, accessories, applicable taxes, transport, installation and any training. Confirm lead times, payment milestones, return terms and warranty coverage in writing.

Discuss your requirements →
PROCUREMENT GUIDE · 03

Build a facility equipment list

Keep your procurement list clear and easy to review.

Read the checklist

Group requirements by room or department, assign quantities and identify any space or utility constraints. Have your clinical and engineering teams review compatibility and installation needs. Keep delivery and acceptance requirements with each line item.
